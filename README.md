## The joke

> I told my son I'd teach him to tell dad jokes. He said, 'Dad, that's a pretty low bar.' I said, 'Exactly — now limbo under it.'

## How the joke lands

A father offers to teach his son to tell dad jokes, and the son answers with a dad joke of his own: "a pretty low bar" is both a jab at the father's jokes and a literal bar to limbo under. The father then turns the pun back on him — "Exactly — now limbo under it" — so the punchline lands at the very end. It fits the judging criteria: it is a joke a father would tell at the table, the pun is clear on first read, and every word earns its place.
